# Source, Citation, and Copyright Policy

## Purpose

This policy applies to every book, chapter, section, example, notebook, figure, and appendix in the Python for Financial Mathematics & Quantitative Finance series.

## Core Rule

The manuscript must be original writing. External material is used to verify facts, definitions, mathematical conventions, algorithms, software behavior, and historical context—not as text to reproduce.

## Reference Requirements

Each substantive section should be developed with references incorporated from the Draft stage. References should be checked again during Technical + Source Review and retained in the Revised Final / Canonical Edition.

Prefer authoritative sources where appropriate, including official Python and scientific-computing documentation, peer-reviewed or university material, standards and regulatory publications, original research papers, and established finance/mathematics texts.

## Quotations and Paraphrasing

Avoid unnecessary quotation. When quotation is genuinely useful, keep it short, clearly mark it as quoted material, and provide attribution. Do not reproduce substantial copyrighted passages, tables, figures, exercises, or proprietary examples without appropriate permission or a compatible license.

Paraphrasing must be genuinely original in wording and structure while accurately representing the source. A citation does not make close copying acceptable.

## Mathematics and Formulas

Standard mathematical facts and conventional formulas may be explained independently. When presenting a named model, theorem, algorithm, empirical result, or specialized derivation, cite an appropriate primary or authoritative source where practical.

Derivations should be written independently for this series and should identify assumptions and notation explicitly.

## Python and Software

Prefer original implementations written for the book. When APIs or behavior are described, cite official project documentation where practical. Third-party code must not be copied into the manuscript unless its license permits the intended use and attribution/license obligations are satisfied.

## Figures, Tables, and Data

Create original figures and tables whenever possible. If external data is used, identify the data source and applicable terms. Do not redraw a copyrighted figure so closely that it effectively reproduces the original without permission.

## Editorial Gate

A section cannot be designated **Revised Final / Canonical Edition** until:

1. technical claims have been reviewed;
2. source-dependent claims have appropriate references;
3. references have been checked for relevance and authority;
4. suspiciously close wording has been rewritten or properly quoted;
5. externally sourced code, figures, tables, and datasets have been checked for license/usage requirements; and
6. the verified reference list is retained in the canonical manuscript.

This process reduces copyright and attribution risk but is an editorial policy, not a substitute for legal advice when publication rights or licensing questions require counsel.
