The validation matrix is substantially exercised for endpoint, zero derivative, invalid sign bracket, nonfinite objective, exhausted budget, adjacent floats and nonpositive absolute tolerance. Reported diagnostics are useful; numeric oracles remain two financial formulas plus sqrt2 fallback.
