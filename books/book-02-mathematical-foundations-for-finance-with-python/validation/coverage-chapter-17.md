## Project, applied problems and independence

The17.9 verifier recomputes objective/gradient and all four KKT residual families from a proposed candidate; negative tests exercise cap, stationarity and dual-sign failures. This is a useful separation of optimizer and verifier functions, but they live in the same implementation, share constants/gradient helpers, and are not an independent human review. The fixed covariance's convexity is known by the manuscript derivation; the project certificate does not accept arbitrary covariance matrices or perform a new matrix-level PD validation.

The project twelve-step lab includes assumption register, covariance validation and parsing inputs. Implementation fixes SIGMA/RETURNS and accepts numerical caps/current holdings/capital/cost; no general parser, dates, asset labels, covariance ingestion or model-version register exists. Financial outputs omit some requested provenance/tolerance metadata; reproducible JSON/schema/serialized determinism is unasserted. Some returned arithmetic lacks explicit finite-result validation despite finite inputs; no extreme capital/weight/metric range evidence is provided.

17.10 extended Problem1 four-regime numbers are well checked; active status at transition/inactive cap is checked more explicitly in17.4. Problem2 second-cap weights/variance are checked, but its expected multiplier0.016 is absent from final helper (17.9 tests only positive second multiplier). Problem3 false-success cap violation and nonstationarity are covered17.8/17.9, not final suite. Problem4 model/execution risk requires manual review.

17.5 zero-gap dual certificate independently recomputes primal/dual equations, while17.10 LP report starts from hardcoded vertices and dual pair. It does not discover them or test invalid LP candidates. Successful fixed reference certificates do not establish general convex solver correctness.

[Earlier cap feasibility review](chapter-17-feasibility-review.md) corrected17.9 so certificate tolerance cannot turn an empty feasible interval into a nonempty one. The tiny-empty regression exists in17.9. Other cap helpers use their own sum/interval conditions, and verification tolerances combine weight, multiplier, stationarity and complementarity quantities. Broader adjacent-boundary, tolerance-scaling and degenerate-multiplier tests remain open.
