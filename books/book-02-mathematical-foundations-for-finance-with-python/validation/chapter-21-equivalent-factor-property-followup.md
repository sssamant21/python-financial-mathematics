INT-01 now states its finite converted-rate/positive-factor domain and separates mathematical equivalence from conversion rounding. Let c be high-precision log1p of the represented effective rate i, and c_hat the returned float. The exact factor ratio is exp(−(c_hat−c)T). The allowance used in the selected checks is the effective factor times abs(expm1(−(c_hat−c)T)), plus one-half ULP of each returned factor. This follows directly from the two discount exponents and the triangle inequality for final rounding.
