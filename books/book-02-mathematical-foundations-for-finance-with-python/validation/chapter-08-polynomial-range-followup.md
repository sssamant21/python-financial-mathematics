**64 checks pass**, with 22 baseline mismatches and a byte-identical baseline replay retained. Those mismatches include numerical results, exception contracts and callback side effects; they are not a count of distinct defects.

The oracle directly sums exact rational monomials and their independently differentiated terms. The implementation instead uses exact rational Horner evaluation with one final binary64 rounding. Twelve explicit vectors and twelve deterministic seeded vectors cover overflow cancellation, subnormal sums, both signs, zero/constant derivatives, finite point-specific derivatives with unrepresentable coefficients, and true final overflow. Eight invalid-input/coefficient observations and seven actual centered-difference behavior checks complete the corpus (48 + 9 + 7 = 64).

The coefficient-returning API now raises OverflowError rather than returning infinity. The point-specific derivative does not require its intermediate coefficients to fit in binary64. Exact zero normalizes to positive zero; nonzero negative underflow may produce negative zero. Input type/domain guards remain TypeError/ValueError. The Section 8.2 centered wrapper delegates to the guarded Section 8.1 helper, retaining its mandatory explicit step; actual calls check collapsed controls before callbacks, nonfinite sampling/results, complete overflowing sampled quotients and negative steps. Inherited mathematics is not counted as a new independent oracle.
