## Project and lab contract

The15.9 twelve-step lab has evidence for reference valuation, all Jacobian/Hessian entries, first order, exact revaluation, centered columns and domain rejection. Symbolic derivation and units (steps2,4,6) remain manual. Step9's second-order contraction is implemented but nonzero reference expected values are not independently asserted; a zero-shock test does not cover cross terms. Step10 requests signed/absolute errors for outputs and total. Implementation reports signed/absolute output errors and portfolio changes, but no explicit portfolio error fields; checks do not assert absolute-error fields. These should be reconciled in final revision.

15.10 imports15.7–15.9 reports, so it shares their oracle/dependency limits; its polynomial entries are literals. Its recursive finite checker deliberately allows boolean status fields, unlike numeric-input validators that reject booleans. This is a distinct role, not an inconsistency. The twelve hands-on steps require deriving/validating Jacobian/Hessians, while the final suite does not independently repeat every underlying assertion.

Finite differences are checked at one step per input, not a systematic truncation/roundoff study. [Boundary regressions](../chapter-15/code/check_finite_difference_boundaries.py) verify unrepresentable rate steps, centered time domain violation, normal analytic column agreement and denominator overflow in15.5/15.9. See [focused review](chapter-15-derivative-boundary-review.md). They do not apply to15.2's separate centered-rate helper, which only checks h>0 and lacks the same representability/2h guards. Perturbation asymmetry, Hessian numerical oracles and extreme valuation cancellation remain open.

Covariance helpers in15.4/15.6 require PD rather than all PSD matrices and tolerate small off-diagonal differences without consistently symmetrizing them. Their raw determinant tests and gradients/Hessians require numerical/contract review. These are narrower interfaces than the general symmetric/PSD mathematical identities.

Principal/cash-flow domains vary intentionally or by teaching scope:15.1 PV permits signed cash flows;15.2 restricts amount nonnegative;15.8/15.9 require strictly positive principal. Cross-section consistency must be reviewed without assuming every restriction is an implementation defect.
