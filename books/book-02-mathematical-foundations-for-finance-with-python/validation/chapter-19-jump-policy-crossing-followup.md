Terminal simulation now compares the elapsed crossing before rounding its reported timestamp and advances the balance over the exact difference between represented endpoints. A strictly later deposit cannot rescue the account. At exact coincidence the scheduled jump is applied to a zero balance before the post-jump check, so a deposit can rescue it. Time-zero rescue and signed nonterminal continuation remain supported. This differs from Section 19.9's depletion-first policy at exact coincidence. Terminal mode rejects nonzero segment balances that round to zero; nonterminal mode may round to zero without declaring depletion.

The linear interpolator evaluates the complete convex combination of the two time endpoints before rounding.
