## Chapter 03 cumulative assessment

Work through these problems using exact arithmetic, explicit domains, and documented units. All amounts are hypothetical.

### Part A — Functions (25 points)

1. For \(f(q)=12q-30\), \(q\in\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}\), list the range and extrema. (5)
2. Let \(g(x)=2x+5\) and \(h(x)=x/2\). Evaluate \(h(g(10))\) and \(g(h(10))\). (5)
3. Invert \(\Pi(q)=8q-40\) on nonnegative whole-unit quantities. Can target profits 24 and 20 be attained? (5)
4. Explain why \(s(x)=x^2\) has no inverse over all real numbers; restrict to \(x\ge0\) and derive the inverse. (5)
5. Distinguish an inverse function from the reciprocal of a function. (5)

### Part B — Financial applications (30 points)

6. A fee is 1% on the first 100 currency units and 3% only on the excess. Write the piecewise formula, evaluate at 100 and 150, and check continuity. (6)
7. An all-units price is 15 for fewer than 10 items and 12 for 10 or more. Evaluate revenue at 9, 10, and 11 and assess monotonicity. (6)
8. Contribution margin is 10, base fixed cost 50, and additional fixed cost 40 applies when quantity exceeds 5. Find profit at 5, 6, and 9. (6)
9. For \(\Pi(q)=7q-21\), \(q\in\{0,\ldots,8\}\), prove monotonicity and find extrema. (6)
10. With \(C(q)=q^2+4q+20\) and \(R(q)=14q\), maximize profit for whole-unit \(0\le q\le10\). (6)

### Part C — Contracts and validation (20 points)

11. State the valid domain and a postcondition invariant for \(PV(C,r)=C/(1+r)\), assuming \(C\ge0\). Compute \(PV(110,1/10)\). (5)
12. Design boundary tests for a fixed cost of 50 through quantity 5 and 90 afterward. Include invalid input tests. (5)
13. Calculate first and second differences for \(3,6,11,18,27\). State what discrete curvature does and does not establish. (5)
14. Give one example each of a domain error, representation error, and financial-model assumption that may be unrealistic. (5)

### Part D — Integrated project (25 points)

15. With price 25, unit cost 15, fixed cost 40, capacity 8, and additional cost 30 only above quantity 5: define revenue, cost, and profit; generate the complete table; identify break-even quantities, all maximizers, and a monotonicity counterexample; and specify two test invariants. (20)
16. Introduce an all-units price of 23 at quantity 7 or more, otherwise 25. Write the revised revenue formula and identify essential boundary tests. (5)

## Worked solutions

1. Range: \(\{-30,-18,-6,6,18,30\}\); minimum -30 at 0, maximum 30 at 5.
2. \(h(g(10))=25/2\), whereas \(g(h(10))=15\).
3. \(q=(\Pi+40)/8\); 24 requires 8 items, while 20 requires 7.5 and is not feasible.
4. \(s(-2)=s(2)=4\); on \(x\ge0\), \(s^{-1}(y)=\sqrt y\) for \(y\ge0\).
5. The inverse reverses input and output; the reciprocal computes \(1/f(x)\).
6. \(F(A)=A/100\) for \(A\le100\), and \(F(A)=1+3(A-100)/100\) above 100. Values are 1 and \(5/2\); continuous at 100.
7. Revenues are 135, 120, 132; not nondecreasing because the 9-to-10 transition drops.
8. \(\Pi(q)=10q-50-40\mathbf1_{\{q>5\}}\); values 0, -30, 0.
9. The difference \(7(q_2-q_1)>0\) for \(q_2>q_1\); minimum -21 at 0, maximum 35 at 8.
10. \(\Pi(q)=-q^2+10q-20=-(q-5)^2+5\); unique maximum 5 at quantity 5.
11. Require \(C\ge0\), \(r>-1\). Invariant \(PV(C,r)(1+r)=C\); result 100.
12. Test quantities 4, 5, 6, 0, -1, and \(1/2\). The first four verify boundaries and minimum; the last two must be rejected.
13. First differences: 3, 5, 7, 9; second differences: 2, 2, 2. Strict discrete convexity on this grid does not prove arbitrary continuous interpolation is convex.
14. Rate -1 is invalid; float 0.1 violates an exact-rational representation contract; fixed unit cost may not represent capacity effects.
15. \(R(q)=25q\), \(C(q)=40+15q+30\mathbf1_{\{q>5\}}\), and \(\Pi=R-C\).

| q | Revenue | Cost | Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | 40 | -40 |
| 1 | 25 | 55 | -30 |
| 2 | 50 | 70 | -20 |
| 3 | 75 | 85 | -10 |
| 4 | 100 | 100 | 0 |
| 5 | 125 | 115 | 10 |
| 6 | 150 | 160 | -10 |
| 7 | 175 | 175 | 0 |
| 8 | 200 | 190 | 10 |

Break-even quantities: 4 and 7. Maximum profit: 10, attained at both 5 and 8. Profit drops from 10 to -10 at the capacity step. Invariants: \(R-C=\Pi\) and \(\Delta\Pi(q)=\Pi(q+1)-\Pi(q)\).

16. \(R(q)=25q\) for \(q<7\), and \(R(q)=23q\) for \(q\ge7\). Test 6, 7, and 8, alongside the separate cost threshold.
