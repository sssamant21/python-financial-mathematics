## Part F

48. Examples: continuous PV of 100 at \(\ln2\) for one year equals 50; zero-rate account balance \(1000+200(3)=1600\).
49. Splitting a cash flow at the same date preserves PV under the same deterministic discount convention and linear valuation.
50. The zero function preserves scaling and additivity but fails nonzero known-value cases.
51. Illustrative math.isclose absolute tolerance \(10^{-9}\) currency units and relative tolerance \(10^{-12}\); suitability depends on numerical error, scale, units and materiality.
52. Exact source and dirty state, input data and assumptions, environment and dependencies, command, timestamp, output, exit code and tolerance.
53. Authored: source exists. Executed: run with recorded outcome. Reviewed: an identified reviewer assessed a defined scope. Approved: an authorized decision for a stated use and version.
54. A seed supports replay under documented conditions; it does not prove distributional assumptions, independence, coverage or adequate sample size.
55. Include inputs, conventions and units for valuation, sensitivity, replication, allocation and account; show diagnostics, limitations, source and evidence status. Mark unperformed checks and review as pending.
56. Example: effective annual 25% treated as continuous produces an incorrect factor relative to the stated effective contract. Record source, inputs, expected and actual output and impact; correct convention handling and independently rerun benchmark and regression checks on the corrected version.
57. Hold the release decision pending review and record the test scope and remaining gate; any exception must follow applicable authority and policy.
58. Specify a distribution and dependence structure, and explain the probability measure and risk assumptions before interpreting an expected discounted amount as a price.
