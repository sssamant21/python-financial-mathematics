## Twenty chapter-wide problems and answers

### Problem 01 — Time-value comparison

**Task:** You may receive 5,000 USD today or 5,500 USD one year later. At 8% effective annual, which has the larger present value?

**Answer and method:** PV of later receipt = 5500/1.08 = 5092.5926 USD, so the later receipt has 92.5926 USD greater present value.

### Problem 02 — Timeline

**Task:** Represent -2,000 USD at year 0, +900 at year 1, and +1,400 at year 3. State the valuation date.

**Answer and method:** Events: (0,-2000), (1,900), (3,1400). Valuation date is year 0.

### Problem 03 — Simple interest

**Task:** Find the future value of 2,500 USD at 6% simple annual interest for 3 years.

**Answer and method:** 2500(1+.06*3) = 2,950 USD.

### Problem 04 — Compound interest

**Task:** Find the future value of 2,500 USD at 6% effective annual for 3 years.

**Answer and method:** 2500(1.06)^3 = 2,977.54 USD (rounded).

### Problem 05 — Present value

**Task:** Discount 8,000 USD due in four years at 5% effective annual.

**Answer and method:** 8000/(1.05)^4 ≈ 6,581.62 USD.

### Problem 06 — Future value

**Task:** Accumulate 3,000 USD for five years at 4% effective annual.

**Answer and method:** 3000(1.04)^5 ≈ 3,649.96 USD.

### Problem 07 — Discount factor

**Task:** Find the three-year discount factor at 7% effective annual.

**Answer and method:** (1.07)^(-3) ≈ 0.81629788.

### Problem 08 — Multiple cash flows

**Task:** Value 100 USD at year 1 and 200 USD at year 2 using 10% effective annual.

**Answer and method:** 100/1.1 + 200/1.1² ≈ 256.1983 USD.

### Problem 09 — NPV decision

**Task:** A project costs 1,000 USD now and pays 600 USD after one year and 600 USD after two years. Use 10% effective annual.

**Answer and method:** NPV = -1000 + 600/1.1 + 600/1.1² ≈ 41.3223 USD. Positive under the stated deterministic model.

### Problem 10 — Effective vs nominal

**Task:** Find the effective annual rate of 12% nominal annually convertible monthly.

**Answer and method:** (1+.12/12)^12-1 ≈ 12.682503%.

### Problem 11 — Frequency comparison

**Task:** Compare 6% nominal annually compounded once versus monthly over one year on 1,000 USD.

**Answer and method:** Annual: 1,060 USD. Monthly: 1000(1+.06/12)^12 ≈ 1,061.6778 USD.

### Problem 12 — Continuous compounding

**Task:** Find FV of 1,500 USD at 5% continuous for two years.

**Answer and method:** 1500 exp(.05*2) ≈ 1,657.7564 USD.

### Problem 13 — Solve for rate

**Task:** An investment grows from 4,000 to 4,840 USD in two years. Find the effective annual rate.

**Answer and method:** (4840/4000)^(1/2)-1 = 10%.

### Problem 14 — Solve for time

**Task:** How many modeled years to double at 8% effective annual?

**Answer and method:** ln(2)/ln(1.08) ≈ 9.00647 years; if only complete annual credits count, the first meeting date is year 10.

### Problem 15 — Rate convention audit

**Task:** Convert 10% effective annual to continuous annual and 12-times nominal annual.

**Answer and method:** Continuous: ln(1.10) ≈ 9.531018%; nominal monthly: 12[(1.10)^(1/12)-1] ≈ 9.5690%.

### Problem 16 — Zero-rate edge

**Task:** At zero interest, what is the PV of 2,000 USD due in five years, and can time be inferred from equal balances?

**Answer and method:** PV = 2,000 USD. Equal balances and zero rate do not identify a unique time.

### Problem 17 — Negative-rate edge

**Task:** What is the FV of 1,000 USD after two years at -5% effective annual?

**Answer and method:** 1000(0.95)^2 = 902.50 USD.

### Problem 18 — Signed-flow reconciliation

**Task:** Three year-zero cash flows are -100, +40, +60 USD. Find NPV at any valid discount rate.

**Answer and method:** NPV = 0; discount factor at year zero is one.

### Problem 19 — CSV audit

**Task:** Specify a CSV header and three rows for -1,000 at year 0, +400 at year 1, +800 at year 2.

**Answer and method:** Header year,amount; rows 0,-1000; 1,400; 2,800. Treat every row as a dated signed event.

### Problem 20 — Integrated valuation

**Task:** Compute NPV for -1,000 now, +400 at year 1, +500 at year 2, +300 at year 3, with 8% nominal monthly compounding.

**Answer and method:** NPV = -1000 + 400/(1+.08/12)^12 + 500/(1+.08/12)^24 + 300/(1+.08/12)^36; evaluate without early rounding.

"""Chapter 9: 20 independent reference checks (standard library only)."""
import math
import unittest

def fv(p, rate, years, m=1):
    if m <= 0 or rate/m <= -1 or years < 0:
        raise ValueError("invalid growth convention")
    return p * math.exp(years*m*math.log1p(rate/m))

def pv(f, rate, years, m=1):
    if m <= 0 or rate/m <= -1 or years < 0:
        raise ValueError("invalid discount convention")
    return f * math.exp(-years*m*math.log1p(rate/m))

def npv(flows, rate, m=1):
    return math.fsum(pv(amount, rate, year, m) for year, amount in flows)

class ChapterNineChecks(unittest.TestCase):

## Common failure modes

- Treating 6% as \`6\` instead of \`0.06\`.
- Applying a nominal annual rate as though it were an effective annual rate.
- Discounting all cash flows as if they occurred at the same date.
- Subtracting the initial investment twice in NPV.
- Rounding rates before accumulation or inverse checks.
- Interpreting fractional modeled periods as actual contractual posting dates.
- Claiming an investment forecast from a deterministic historical equivalent rate.
- Assuming a zero-rate inverse-time calculation always has a unique answer.
