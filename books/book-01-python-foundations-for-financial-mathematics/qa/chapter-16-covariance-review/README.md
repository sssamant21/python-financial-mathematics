Required float outputs raise ValueError when nonfinite or when a nonzero exact value would become zero. Two rows remain required even for population estimation. General signed exposures are accepted by the quadratic form, without a unit-budget or financing claim.

Matrix policy: symmetry accepts only a stated pair-relative plus diagonal-scale tolerance and averages accepted pairs. Negative diagonal variances are rejected; zero-variance rows must have exactly zero covariance. A full rational Schur-complement screen is applied to the matrix plus delta times identity, where delta is 1e-12 times the largest diagonal. This establishes a scale-relative screen, not exact PSD certification. Tiny negative quadratic forms within delta times squared exposure norm become zero; larger negatives are rejected. Correlations are bounded separately, with at most 1e-12 overshoot clipped. Tiny-unit invalid matrices are tested so a fixed absolute tolerance cannot hide their negative risk. Near-singular rounding, performance and empirical estimator suitability remain limits.
