The worked 1000-to-1210 example is two years. Doubling at 7% takes 10.2447683511 modeled years; exact rational powers bracket the first annual credit at 11. The nominal 6% monthly example recovers two years from 24 periods. Continuous annual growth requires the continuous quote, while periodic time is in periods. No additions, withdrawals, fees or changing rates are modeled. Calendar/day-count rules, credit cutoffs and market-model acceptance are outside this review. Same-sign negative balances satisfy the algebra; economic interpretation still depends on the liability contract.
