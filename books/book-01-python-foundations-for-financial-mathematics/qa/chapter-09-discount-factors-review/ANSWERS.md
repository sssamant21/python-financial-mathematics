The worked 4% grid uses factors (25/26)^n for n=0,1,2,3; all four factors and corresponding 5000-payment values were reconciled. The ratio D(0,5)/D(0,2) gives D(2,5), while the reversed ratio is accumulation. At a −2% rate the one-period discount factor is 50/49 > 1, illustrating why a factor need not be below one.
