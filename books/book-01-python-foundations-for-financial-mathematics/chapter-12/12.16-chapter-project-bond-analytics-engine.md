## Project objective

Build a small, auditable **fixed-income analytics engine** using only the Python standard library. The engine must connect bond cash-flow generation, yield-to-maturity inversion, flat-yield pricing, spot-curve pricing, Macaulay duration, modified duration, convexity, DV01, scenario repricing, and portfolio aggregation.

This capstone integrates Sections 12.1–12.15. It is an educational reference implementation, not a production trading, accounting, or risk-management system.

## 1. Architecture and scope

```text
Bond terms
   |
   +--> Contractual cash flows --> Flat-YTM price --> Duration / convexity / DV01
   |                                    |
   |                                    +--> YTM solver
   |                                    +--> Yield-shock scenarios
   |
   +--> Spot-curve discount factors --> Spot-curve price
                                            |
Portfolio positions ------------------------+--> Scenario aggregation
```

The reference engine handles **fixed-rate bullet bonds and zero-coupon bonds** with equally spaced coupon periods. The bond's `periods` input is the number of **remaining coupon payments**, not the number of years. A three-year semiannual bond therefore has six periods.

The engine excludes irregular first/last coupons, actual calendar settlement, accrued interest, day-count conventions, amortizing principal, defaults, embedded options, taxes, and transaction costs. These are essential extensions for production applications.

## 2. Requirements and acceptance contract

| Capability | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Bond validation | Positive face value; nonnegative coupon; positive integer periods and frequency |
| Cash-flow schedule | Coupon at each period; principal added to final payment |
| Flat-YTM pricing | Nominal annual YTM compounded at coupon frequency |
| YTM inversion | Bisection with an explicit bracketing interval |
| Duration | Macaulay and modified duration |
| Convexity | Analytical second-order yield sensitivity |
| DV01 | Positive loss magnitude for a +1-bp yield move |
| Spot pricing | Annual-effective spot rate at every cash-flow date |
| Scenarios | Exact, duration-only, and duration-plus-convexity P&L |
| Portfolio | Sum individual bond scenario dollar changes |
| Tests | Twelve standard-library reference unit tests |

**Rate convention matters:** The flat-YTM analytics use a **nominal annual yield compounded m times per year**. Spot-curve pricing uses **annual-effective spot rates**. To compare them, convert conventions explicitly.

## 3. Mathematical model

For face value F, annual coupon rate c, coupon frequency m, and remaining periods n:

**Coupon per period = Fc/m.**

Cash flows are **CF(k)=Fc/m** for k=1,…,n−1, and **CF(n)=Fc/m+F**.

The flat-YTM price is:

**P(y)=Σ[k=1..n] CF(k)/(1+y/m)^k.**

Let **PV(k)=CF(k)/(1+y/m)^k**.

Then:

**D_Mac = Σ k×PV(k)/(mP).**

**D_Mod = D_Mac/(1+y/m).**

**Convexity = Σ k(k+1)PV(k)/[m²P(1+y/m)²].**

**DV01 = P×D_Mod×0.0001.**

For a yield change Δy in decimal units:

**ΔP_duration ≈ −P D_Mod Δy.**

**ΔP_duration+convexity ≈ −P D_Mod Δy + 0.5 P Convexity (Δy)².**

Exact repricing uses **P(y+Δy)−P(y)**.

## 4. Yield-to-maturity solver

Given a positive market dirty price, solve **P(y)=market_price** by bisection.

For a conventional fixed-cash-flow bond with positive cash flows, price decreases monotonically with yield on the valid yield domain **1+y/m>0**. This makes bisection robust when the desired root lies inside the supplied bracket.

Algorithm:

1. Validate the market price and bracket endpoints.
2. Evaluate price minus market price at both endpoints.
3. Reject a bracket that does not contain a root.
4. Bisect repeatedly and retain the half-interval containing the root.
5. Stop when the absolute price residual meets tolerance.

The implementation defaults to a wide educational bracket, but the solver must reject roots outside it rather than silently return an invented yield.

## 5. Spot-curve valuation

For cash flow CF at time t and annual-effective spot rate s(t):

**DF(t)=[1+s(t)]^(−t).**

**P_spot=Σ CF(t)DF(t).**

The engine requires a spot rate at **every exact cash-flow time** and raises an error when a node is missing. It does not silently interpolate or extrapolate. Curve construction and interpolation policy should be explicit in a larger system.

## 6. Worked example — Par coupon bond

Use:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---:|
| Face | $1,000 |
| Coupon rate | 6% annually |
| Frequency | Semiannual |
| Remaining periods | 6 |
| YTM | 6% nominal annual, semiannual compounding |

The six payments occur at 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, and 3.0 years.

Each regular coupon is **$30**; the final cash flow is **$1,030**.

Because coupon and YTM match under the same frequency convention, the price is **$1,000**.

The YTM solver should recover **6%** from that price. Modified duration and convexity must both be positive.

## 7. Flat-YTM and spot-rate equivalence

A nominal 6% yield compounded semiannually has a periodic rate of 3%.

Its equivalent annual-effective rate is:

**r_eff=(1+0.06/2)²−1=6.09%.**

Discounting every payment at this **flat annual-effective spot rate** produces the same value as discounting with nominal 6% semiannual YTM, provided cash-flow times align.

This is a critical regression test. Using a 6% annual-effective spot rate directly would not be convention-equivalent.

## 8. Portfolio specification

Build a portfolio of three long positions:

| Bond | Face | Annual coupon | Remaining term | Frequency | Nominal annual YTM |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | $1,000 | 6% | 3 years | 2 | 6% |
| B | $2,000 | 4% | 5 years | 2 | 5% |
| C | $1,500 | 0% | 4 years | 2 | 4.5% |

The reference implementation treats face amounts as **total held face values**, so returned prices and dollar risk measures are position-level amounts.

Calculate each position's price, Macaulay duration, modified duration, convexity, DV01, and recovered YTM.

## 9. Scenario and risk report

For each common nominal-YTM shock **−200, −100, −50, +50, +100, and +200 bp**, calculate:

- Exact dollar P&L.
- Duration-only dollar P&L.
- Duration-plus-convexity dollar P&L.
- Approximation errors relative to exact repricing.
- Portfolio totals as the sum of position-level dollar P&L.

A **parallel YTM shock** is a simplified scenario applied to each bond's quoted yield. It is not identical to a fully specified parallel shift of an arbitrage-free zero curve, and it does not isolate credit-spread movements.

## 10. Python implementation

Save as **bond_analytics_engine.py**. Run `python bond_analytics_engine.py` and `python -m compileall -q bond_analytics_engine.py`.

The reference code is self-contained and uses only `dataclasses`, `math`, and `unittest`. It includes **12 reference tests**. Independent runtime execution and editorial QA remain pending.

```python
from dataclasses import dataclass
from math import exp, fsum, isfinite, log1p
import unittest

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Bond:
    face: float
    coupon: float
    periods: int
    frequency: int

    def __post_init__(self):
        if any(isinstance(v, bool) or not isinstance(v, (int, float)) or not isfinite(v) for v in (self.face, self.coupon)):
            raise ValueError('face and coupon must be finite')
        if self.face <= 0 or self.coupon < 0: raise ValueError('invalid face/coupon')
        if any(isinstance(v, bool) or not isinstance(v, int) or v < 1 for v in (self.periods, self.frequency)):
            raise ValueError('periods and frequency must be positive integers')

    def cashflows(self):
        c = self.face * self.coupon / self.frequency
        return [(k / self.frequency, c + (self.face if k == self.periods else 0.0))
                for k in range(1, self.periods + 1)]

def valid_yield(y, m):
    if isinstance(y, bool) or not isinstance(y, (int, float)) or not isfinite(y) or y / m <= -1:
        raise ValueError('invalid yield')

def analytics(bond, y):
    valid_yield(y, bond.frequency)
    m = bond.frequency; q = y / m; L = log1p(q)
    rows = [(k, amount * exp(-k * L)) for k, (_, amount) in enumerate(bond.cashflows(), 1)]
    p = fsum(v for _, v in rows)
    weighted = fsum(k * v for k, v in rows)
    second_weighted = fsum(k * (k + 1) * v for k, v in rows)
    macaulay = weighted / (m * p)
    modified = macaulay / (1 + q)
    convexity = second_weighted / (m * m * (1 + q)**2 * p)
    return {'price': p, 'macaulay': macaulay, 'modified': modified,
            'convexity': convexity, 'dv01': p * modified * 1e-4}

def solve_ytm(bond, market_price, low=-0.90, high=1.0, tolerance=1e-11):
    if isinstance(market_price, bool) or not isinstance(market_price, (int, float)) or not isfinite(market_price) or market_price <= 0:
        raise ValueError('market price must be positive and finite')
    valid_yield(low, bond.frequency); valid_yield(high, bond.frequency)
    if low >= high: raise ValueError('invalid bracket')
    f_low = analytics(bond, low)['price'] - market_price
    f_high = analytics(bond, high)['price'] - market_price
    if f_low * f_high > 0: raise ValueError('yield not bracketed')
    if f_low == 0: return low
    if f_high == 0: return high
    for _ in range(200):
        mid = (low + high) / 2
        error = analytics(bond, mid)['price'] - market_price
        if abs(error) <= tolerance: return mid
        if error > 0: low = mid
        else: high = mid
    return (low + high) / 2

def spot_price(bond, spot_by_time):
    total = []
    for t, amount in bond.cashflows():
        if t not in spot_by_time: raise ValueError('missing spot node at cashflow date')
        r = spot_by_time[t]
        if isinstance(r, bool) or not isinstance(r, (int, float)) or not isfinite(r) or r <= -1:
            raise ValueError('invalid annual-effective spot rate')
        total.append(amount * exp(-t * log1p(r)))
    return fsum(total)

def scenario(bond, y, shock_bp):
    if isinstance(shock_bp, bool) or not isinstance(shock_bp, (int, float)) or not isfinite(shock_bp):
        raise ValueError('invalid shock')
    base = analytics(bond, y)
    dy = shock_bp / 10000
    shocked = analytics(bond, y + dy)['price']
    linear = -base['price'] * base['modified'] * dy
    quadratic = linear + 0.5 * base['price'] * base['convexity'] * dy**2
    return {'exact': shocked - base['price'], 'duration': linear,
            'duration_convexity': quadratic, 'shocked_price': shocked}

def portfolio_scenario(positions, shock_bp):
    if not positions: raise ValueError('positions required')
    rows = [scenario(bond, y, shock_bp) for bond, y in positions]
    return {key: fsum(row[key] for row in rows) for key in ('exact', 'duration', 'duration_convexity')}

class EngineTests(unittest.TestCase):
    def setUp(self): self.b = Bond(1000, .06, 6, 2)
    def test_01_cashflows(self):
        c = self.b.cashflows(); self.assertEqual(len(c), 6); self.assertEqual(c[0], (.5, 30)); self.assertEqual(c[-1], (3, 1030))
    def test_02_par_price(self): self.assertAlmostEqual(analytics(self.b, .06)['price'], 1000)
    def test_03_ytm_roundtrip(self): self.assertAlmostEqual(solve_ytm(self.b, 1000), .06, places=9)
    def test_04_discount_price(self): self.assertGreater(analytics(self.b, .07)['price'], 0)
    def test_05_macaulay(self): self.assertGreater(analytics(self.b, .06)['macaulay'], 0)
    def test_06_modified(self):
        a = analytics(self.b, .06); self.assertAlmostEqual(a['modified'], a['macaulay']/1.03)
    def test_07_convexity(self): self.assertGreater(analytics(self.b, .06)['convexity'], 0)
    def test_08_dv01(self):
        a = analytics(self.b, .06); self.assertAlmostEqual(a['dv01'], a['price'] * a['modified'] / 10000)
    def test_09_spot_flat_equivalence(self):
        spots = {k / 2: (1.03**2 - 1) for k in range(1, 7)}
        self.assertAlmostEqual(spot_price(self.b, spots), 1000)
    def test_10_scenario_improvement(self):
        s = scenario(self.b, .06, 100)
        self.assertLess(abs(s['exact'] - s['duration_convexity']), abs(s['exact'] - s['duration']))
    def test_11_portfolio_additivity(self):
        a = scenario(self.b, .06, 50); p = portfolio_scenario([(self.b, .06), (self.b, .06)], 50)
        self.assertAlmostEqual(p['exact'], 2*a['exact'])
    def test_12_validation(self):
        for fn in (lambda: Bond(0, .06, 6, 2), lambda: Bond(1000, -.01, 6, 2),
                   lambda: analytics(self.b, -2), lambda: solve_ytm(self.b, -1),
                   lambda: spot_price(self.b, {1: .04}), lambda: scenario(self.b, .06, float('nan'))):
            with self.assertRaises(ValueError): fn()

if __name__ == '__main__':
    portfolio = [(Bond(1000, .06, 6, 2), .06), (Bond(2000, .04, 10, 2), .05),
                 (Bond(1500, 0, 8, 2), .045)]
    for index, (bond, y) in enumerate(portfolio, 1):
        a = analytics(bond, y)
        print('Bond', index, {k: round(v, 6) for k, v in a.items()})
        print('Recovered YTM:', round(solve_ytm(bond, a['price']), 8))
    for bp in (-200, -100, -50, 50, 100, 200):
        print('Portfolio shock', bp, 'bp', portfolio_scenario(portfolio, bp))
    unittest.main(verbosity=2)
```

## 11. Interpreting the output

A well-formed report should show:

1. Each bond's base price and risk metrics.
2. A recovered YTM that agrees with its original input yield.
3. Positive modified duration and convexity for the conventional long-only bonds.
4. Positive DV01 magnitudes.
5. Negative exact price changes when yields rise, and positive changes when yields fall.
6. A portfolio scenario total that equals the sum of individual position changes.
7. An error comparison showing the effect of adding convexity.

Duration-plus-convexity generally improves approximation accuracy for modest shocks to fixed-cash-flow bonds, but the improvement is not guaranteed for every conceivable instrument or shock.

**Numerical checks:** Par price, YTM round trip, compounding-equivalence test, analytical duration and convexity signs, DV01 formula, and scenario additivity.

## 13. Operational failure scenarios

| Failure | Likely cause | Correct response |
|---|---|---|
| YTM solver cannot bracket root | Price outside yields covered by bracket | Check market price, conventions, and bracket |
| Missing spot rate | Curve has no matching cash-flow node | Supply an explicit interpolation/calibration policy |
| Unexpected par-price mismatch | Coupon and yield conventions differ | Normalize compounding and coupon frequency |
| DV01 off by 100× | Basis points confused with percent | Use 1 bp = 0.0001 |
| Portfolio P&L mismatch | Unit face versus held face confusion | Standardize position notional and units |
| Negative convexity on conventional bond | Derivative or cash-flow bug | Inspect second-derivative implementation |
| Large duration approximation error | Shock too large for linearization | Use exact repricing and inspect convexity |
| Non-finite price | Invalid yield domain or inputs | Reject before valuation |
