## Formula reference

- Level payment: **A=Lr/[1-(1+r)^(-n)]**, for r>0; **A=L/n** when r=0.
- Interest in period k: **I(k)=r×B(k-1)**.
- Principal reduction: **P(k)=Payment(k)-I(k)**.
- Closing balance: **B(k)=B(k-1)-P(k)**.
- Prospective balance: **B(k)=A[1-(1+r)^(-(n-k))]/r** when r>0.
- Mortgage LTV: **Loan amount / Property value**.
- Effective annual rate: **(1+r)^m-1**.
- Net loan proceeds with withheld fees F: **L-F**.
- Effective borrowing-cost IRR: **L-F=sum of discounted loan repayments**.

Unless specified otherwise, assume a fixed periodic rate, end-of-period installments, no fees, and full precision until display.

## Part A — Loan foundations, amortization, mortgages, and refinancing

### Exercise 1 — Rate conversion

**Problem:** Nominal annual 6% compounded monthly: find periodic rate.

**Worked answer:** 0.06/12=0.005, or 0.5% per month.

### Exercise 2 — Payment count

**Problem:** How many monthly installments in 15 and 30 years?

**Worked answer:** 15×12=180; 30×12=360.

### Exercise 3 — Zero interest

**Problem:** Repay $1,200 over 12 periods with no interest.

**Worked answer:** A=1200/12=$100, all principal.

### Exercise 4 — Fixed payment

**Problem:** Find payment for $10,000 at 0.5% monthly for 24 months.

**Worked answer:** A=10000×.005/[1-(1.005)^(-24)]≈$443.21.

### Exercise 5 — First interest

**Problem:** Find first interest on the loan in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** 10000×.005=$50.

### Exercise 6 — First principal

**Problem:** Find first principal in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** A-50≈$393.21.

### Exercise 7 — First closing balance

**Problem:** Find balance after first payment in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** 10000×1.005-A≈$9,606.79.

### Exercise 8 — Prospective balance

**Problem:** Express balance after ten payments in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** B(10)=A[1-(1.005)^(-14)]/.005.

### Exercise 9 — Retrospective balance

**Problem:** Give an alternative expression for Exercise 8.

**Worked answer:** B(10)=10000(1.005)^10-A[((1.005)^10-1)/.005].

### Exercise 10 — Schedule invariants

**Problem:** Name three row-level reconciliation checks.

**Worked answer:** Payment=Interest+Principal; Closing=Opening-Principal; next Opening=prior Closing.

### Exercise 11 — Total interest

**Problem:** How is lifetime interest computed without fees?

**Worked answer:** Sum of actual payments minus original principal; for n exact equal payments, nA-L.

### Exercise 12 — Down payment

**Problem:** A $300,000 home with $60,000 down and no financed fees: principal?

**Worked answer:** $240,000.

### Exercise 13 — Loan-to-value

**Problem:** Calculate LTV for Exercise 12.

**Worked answer:** 240000/300000=80%.

### Exercise 14 — Mortgage payment

**Problem:** Find monthly principal-and-interest on $240,000 at 6% nominal, 30 years.

**Worked answer:** A=240000×.005/[1-(1.005)^(-360)]≈$1,438.92.

### Exercise 15 — Housing cost

**Problem:** Add $3,600 annual taxes and $1,200 annual insurance to Exercise 14.

**Worked answer:** $300+$100 per month extra; approximately $1,838.92 monthly total before other costs.

### Exercise 16 — Extra principal

**Problem:** Add $100 principal to first installment in Exercise 4: total and closing balance?

**Worked answer:** Payment≈$543.21; closing≈$9,506.79.

### Exercise 17 — Prepayment mechanism

**Problem:** Why do extra principal payments reduce later interest?

**Worked answer:** Future interest r×B is calculated on a lower balance.

### Exercise 18 — Timing of prepayment

**Problem:** Which saves more interest: same $500 in period 2 or period 20?

**Worked answer:** Period 2 generally saves more because more interest-accrual periods remain.

### Exercise 19 — Refinance payment

**Problem:** Compare $200,000, 240 months at 6% versus 4.5% nominal annual.

**Worked answer:** Savings=A(200000,.06/12,240)-A(200000,.045/12,240)>0.

### Exercise 20 — Simple break-even

**Problem:** Closing costs $3,000 and monthly savings $100.

**Worked answer:** 3000/100=30 months, excluding time value and residual balances.

### Exercise 21 — Holding horizon

**Problem:** Why include remaining principal when comparing refinancing offers?

**Worked answer:** A longer term can lower installments while leaving more debt; use common-horizon cash flows.

### Exercise 22 — Net proceeds

**Problem:** $10,000 loan with $200 fees withheld: proceeds?

**Worked answer:** $9,800.

### Exercise 23 — Borrowing-cost IRR

**Problem:** State IRR equation for Exercise 22 at 0.5% monthly and 24 months.

**Worked answer:** 9800=sum from k=1 to 24 of A(10000,.005,24)/(1+q)^k; q>.005.

### Exercise 24 — Annualization

**Problem:** Convert effective monthly 1% to nominal annualized and effective annual rates.

**Worked answer:** 12% nominal annualized; (1.01)^12-1≈12.6825% effective annual.

### Exercise 25 — Rounding at maturity

**Problem:** Why can a cent-rounded schedule need a different final installment?

**Worked answer:** Accumulated rounding may leave a residual; adjust the final installment to clear principal and interest under the chosen policy.

## Part B — Applied case study: Loan and mortgage decisions

Separately, they consider refinancing a $200,000 balance with 240 payments remaining from 6% to 4.5% nominal annual, with $3,000 cash closing costs.

**Task 1 — Baseline:** Calculate monthly principal and interest, first-month interest, total interest, and payoff month. Use A(240000,.06/12,360).

**Task 2 — Accelerated repayment:** Apply $200 extra principal each month while preserving the original regular installment. Determine interest saved and payoff periods saved. Cap the last installment at the amount due.

**Task 3 — Refinancing:** Calculate the old and new installments on the $200,000 balance, the monthly payment savings, and simple break-even in months. Explain why common-horizon cash-flow analysis is more informative than simple break-even.

**Task 4 — Reconciliation:** Verify that each payment equals interest plus principal, total principal reduction equals the initial balance, and final outstanding principal is zero.

**Worked solution method:** Execute the reference program below. Compare the baseline `schedule(240000,.06/12,360)` with `schedule(240000,.06/12,360,200)`; calculate refinance payments using `payment(200000,.06/12,240)` and `payment(200000,.045/12,240)`. Compute simple break-even as ceiling of $3,000 divided by monthly savings. Use unrounded amounts in the calculations. The program uses only Python's standard library and contains **12 unit tests** for loan formulas, balances, prepayment savings, LTV, effective annualization, and upfront-fee borrowing costs.

```python
import math
import unittest

def payment(L,r,n):
    if not (math.isfinite(L) and L>0 and math.isfinite(r) and r>=0 and isinstance(n,int) and not isinstance(n,bool) and n>0): raise ValueError("invalid loan")
    return L/n if r==0 else L*r/(-math.expm1(-n*math.log1p(r)))

def balance(L,r,n,k):
    a=payment(L,r,n)
    if not isinstance(k,int) or isinstance(k,bool) or not 0<=k<=n: raise ValueError("invalid period")
    if k==n:return 0.
    return a*(n-k) if r==0 else a*(-math.expm1(-(n-k)*math.log1p(r)))/r

def schedule(L,r,n,extra=0):
    a=payment(L,r,n)
    if not math.isfinite(extra) or extra<0:raise ValueError("invalid extra")
    b=L; rows=[]
    for k in range(1,n+1):
        interest=b*r
        paid=min(b+interest,a+extra)
        principal=paid-interest
        closing=max(0.,b-principal)
        if closing<1e-8:closing=0.
        rows.append((k,b,interest,paid,principal,closing))
        b=closing
        if b==0:break
    return rows

def effective_annual(r,m=12):
    if r<0 or m<1:raise ValueError("invalid annualization")
    return math.expm1(m*math.log1p(r))

def borrowing_cost(L,r,n,fee):
    if fee<0 or fee>=L:raise ValueError("invalid fee")
    a=payment(L,r,n)
    if fee==0:return r
    proceeds=L-fee
    def pv(q):return a*n if q==0 else a*(-math.expm1(-n*math.log1p(q)))/q
    lo=r;hi=max(.01,2*r+.01)
    while pv(hi)>proceeds:hi=2*hi+.01
    for _ in range(120):
        mid=(lo+hi)/2
        if pv(mid)>proceeds:lo=mid
        else:hi=mid
    return (lo+hi)/2

class ExerciseChecks(unittest.TestCase):
    def test_01_zero_rate(self):self.assertEqual(payment(1200,0,12),100)
    def test_02_monthly_rate(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(.06/12,.005)
    def test_03_payment(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(payment(10000,.005,24),10000*.005/(1-1.005**-24))
    def test_04_first_interest(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(schedule(10000,.005,24)[0][2],50)
    def test_05_first_principal(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(schedule(10000,.005,24)[0][4],payment(10000,.005,24)-50)
    def test_06_balance(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(balance(10000,.005,24,1),schedule(10000,.005,24)[0][-1])
    def test_07_maturity(self):self.assertEqual(balance(10000,.005,24,24),0)
    def test_08_principal(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(sum(x[4] for x in schedule(10000,.005,24)),10000)
    def test_09_prepayment(self):
        self.assertLess(sum(x[2] for x in schedule(10000,.005,24,100)),sum(x[2] for x in schedule(10000,.005,24)))
    def test_10_ltv(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(240000/300000,.8)
    def test_11_effective(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(effective_annual(.01),1.01**12-1)
    def test_12_fee(self):self.assertGreater(borrowing_cost(10000,.005,24,200),.005)

if __name__=="__main__":
    b=schedule(240000,.06/12,360)
    e=schedule(240000,.06/12,360,200)
    print("Baseline:",len(b),"payments, interest",round(sum(x[2] for x in b),2))
    print("Accelerated:",len(e),"payments, interest",round(sum(x[2] for x in e),2))
    unittest.main(verbosity=2)
```
