## Formula reference

- Level payment: **A=Lr/[1-(1+r)^(-n)]**, for r>0; **A=L/n** when r=0.
- Interest in period k: **I(k)=r×B(k-1)**.
- Principal reduction: **P(k)=Payment(k)-I(k)**.
- Closing balance: **B(k)=B(k-1)-P(k)**.
- Prospective balance: **B(k)=A[1-(1+r)^(-(n-k))]/r** when r>0.
- Mortgage LTV: **Loan amount / Property value**.
- Effective annual rate: **(1+r)^m-1**.
- Net loan proceeds with withheld fees F: **L-F**.
- Effective borrowing-cost IRR: **L-F=sum of discounted loan repayments**.

Unless specified otherwise, assume a fixed periodic rate, end-of-period installments, no fees, and full precision until display.

## Part A — Loan foundations, amortization, mortgages, and refinancing

### Exercise 1 — Rate conversion

**Problem:** Nominal annual 6% compounded monthly: find periodic rate.

**Worked answer:** 0.06/12=0.005, or 0.5% per month.

### Exercise 2 — Payment count

**Problem:** How many monthly installments in 15 and 30 years?

**Worked answer:** 15×12=180; 30×12=360.

### Exercise 3 — Zero interest

**Problem:** Repay $1,200 over 12 periods with no interest.

**Worked answer:** A=1200/12=$100, all principal.

### Exercise 4 — Fixed payment

**Problem:** Find payment for $10,000 at 0.5% monthly for 24 months.

**Worked answer:** A=10000×.005/[1-(1.005)^(-24)]≈$443.21.

### Exercise 5 — First interest

**Problem:** Find first interest on the loan in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** 10000×.005=$50.

### Exercise 6 — First principal

**Problem:** Find first principal in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** A-50≈$393.21.

### Exercise 7 — First closing balance

**Problem:** Find balance after first payment in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** 10000×1.005-A≈$9,606.79.

### Exercise 8 — Prospective balance

**Problem:** Express balance after ten payments in Exercise 4.

**Worked answer:** B(10)=A[1-(1.005)^(-14)]/.005.

### Exercise 9 — Retrospective balance

**Problem:** Give an alternative expression for Exercise 8.

**Worked answer:** B(10)=10000(1.005)^10-A[((1.005)^10-1)/.005].

### Exercise 10 — Schedule invariants

**Problem:** Name three row-level reconciliation checks.

**Worked answer:** Payment=Interest+Principal; Closing=Opening-Principal; next Opening=prior Closing.

### Exercise 11 — Total interest

**Problem:** How is lifetime interest computed without fees?

**Worked answer:** Sum of actual payments minus original principal; for n exact equal payments, nA-L.

### Exercise 12 — Down payment

**Problem:** A $300,000 home with $60,000 down and no financed fees: principal?

**Worked answer:** $240,000.

### Exercise 13 — Loan-to-value

**Problem:** Calculate LTV for Exercise 12.

**Worked answer:** 240000/300000=80%.

### Exercise 14 — Mortgage payment

**Problem:** Find monthly principal-and-interest on $240,000 at 6% nominal compounded monthly, over 30 years.

**Worked answer:** A=240000×.005/[1-(1.005)^(-360)]≈$1,438.92.

### Exercise 15 — Housing cost

**Problem:** Add $3,600 annual taxes and $1,200 annual insurance to Exercise 14.

**Worked answer:** $300+$100 per month extra; approximately $1,838.92 monthly total before other costs.

### Exercise 16 — Extra principal

**Problem:** Add $100 principal to first installment in Exercise 4: total and closing balance?

**Worked answer:** Payment≈$543.21; closing≈$9,506.79.

### Exercise 17 — Prepayment mechanism

**Problem:** Why do extra principal payments reduce later interest?

**Worked answer:** Future interest r×B is calculated on a lower balance.

### Exercise 18 — Timing of prepayment

**Problem:** For the loan in Exercise 4, compare a single $500 principal payment in period 2 versus period 20, keeping the regular installment unchanged.

**Worked answer:** Period 2 saves more: the balance is lower for more interest-accrual periods. In both cases apply the extra after that period’s interest accrues.

### Exercise 19 — Refinance payment

**Problem:** Compare $200,000, 240 months at 6% versus 4.5% nominal annual.

**Worked answer:** The old and new installments are approximately $1,432.86 and $1,265.30. Subtract the unrounded installments for monthly savings of about $167.56.

### Exercise 20 — Simple break-even

**Problem:** Closing costs $3,000 and monthly savings $100.

**Worked answer:** 3000/100=30 months, excluding time value and residual balances.

### Exercise 21 — Holding horizon

**Problem:** Why include remaining principal when comparing refinancing offers?

**Worked answer:** A longer term can lower installments while leaving more debt; use common-horizon cash flows.

### Exercise 22 — Net proceeds

**Problem:** $10,000 loan with $200 fees withheld: proceeds?

**Worked answer:** $9,800.

### Exercise 23 — Borrowing-cost IRR

**Problem:** State IRR equation for Exercise 22 at 0.5% monthly and 24 months.

**Worked answer:** 9800=sum from k=1 to 24 of A(10000,.005,24)/(1+q)^k; q>.005.

### Exercise 24 — Annualization

**Problem:** Convert effective monthly 1% to nominal annualized and effective annual rates.

**Worked answer:** 12% nominal annualized; (1.01)^12-1≈12.6825% effective annual.

### Exercise 25 — Rounding at maturity

**Problem:** Why can a cent-rounded schedule need a different final installment?

**Worked answer:** Accumulated rounding may leave a residual; adjust the final installment to clear principal and interest under the chosen policy.

Use Python 3.9 or later and its standard library; no input files or previous listings are required. Model inputs use their decimal text. The schedule uses exact rational arithmetic before reporting floats, and the borrowing-cost solver evaluates normalized PV in a local high-precision Decimal context. This reference is intended for teaching-sized terms. The program uses only Python's standard library and contains **12 unit tests** for loan formulas, balances, prepayment savings, LTV, effective annualization, and upfront-fee borrowing costs.

```python
import math
import unittest
from decimal import Context, Decimal, DecimalException, localcontext
from fractions import Fraction

def _number(value,label,positive=False):
    if isinstance(value,bool) or not isinstance(value,(int,float)):
        raise ValueError(f"invalid {label}")
    try:
        finite=math.isfinite(value)
    except OverflowError:
        finite=False
    if not finite or value<0 or (positive and value==0):
        raise ValueError(f"invalid {label}")
    return Fraction(str(value))

def _count(n):
    if isinstance(n,bool) or not isinstance(n,int) or n<1:
        raise ValueError("positive integer period count required")

_MIN_FLOAT=Fraction(math.ulp(0.0))

def _report(value,label):
    try:
        if 0<value<_MIN_FLOAT:
            raise OverflowError
        result=float(value)
    except OverflowError as exc:
        raise ValueError(f"{label} outside supported range") from exc
    if not math.isfinite(result):
        raise ValueError(f"{label} outside supported range")
    return result

def _annuity(rate,n):
    return Fraction(n) if rate==0 else (1-(1+rate)**-n)/rate

def payment(principal,rate,periods):
    _count(periods)
    L=_number(principal,"principal",positive=True)
    r=_number(rate,"rate")
    return _report(L/_annuity(r,periods),"payment")

def _proceeds(principal,fees):
    L=_number(principal,"principal",positive=True)
    F=_number(fees,"fees")
    if F>=L:
        raise ValueError("fees must be less than principal")
    return L,F,L-F

def net_proceeds(principal,prepaid_fees):
    return _report(_proceeds(principal,prepaid_fees)[2],"net proceeds")

def balance(L,r,n,k):
    periods,after=n,k
    L=_number(L,"principal",positive=True)
    r=_number(r,"rate")
    if type(periods) is not int or periods<1:
        raise ValueError("periods must be a positive integer")
    if type(after) is not int or not 0<=after<=periods:
        raise ValueError("invalid elapsed periods")
    if after==0:
        return _report(L,"balance")
    if after==periods:
        return 0.0
    return _report(L*_annuity(r,periods-after)/_annuity(r,periods),"balance")

def schedule(L,r,n,extra=0):
    _count(n)
    opening=_number(L,"principal",positive=True)
    rate=_number(r,"rate")
    additional=_number(extra,"extra")
    amount=opening/_annuity(rate,n)
    _report(amount,"regular installment")
    rows=[]
    for k in range(1,n+1):
        interest=opening*rate
        due=opening+interest
        _report(due,"amount due")
        paid=min(due,amount+additional)
        repaid=paid-interest
        closing=due-paid
        rows.append((k,*(_report(v,"schedule amount")
                         for v in (opening,interest,paid,repaid,closing))))
        if closing==0:
            break
        opening=closing
    return rows

def reconciles(rows,principal):
    if not rows or rows[-1][-1]!=0:
        return False
    def close(a,b):
        return math.isclose(a,b,rel_tol=1e-12,abs_tol=0)
    opening=principal
    for _,start,interest,paid,repaid,closing in rows:
        if not (close(start,opening) and close(paid,interest+repaid)
                and close(start,closing+repaid)):
            return False
        opening=closing
    return close(math.fsum(row[4] for row in rows),principal)

def periodic_irr(principal,note_rate,periods,prepaid_fees=0):
    _count(periods)
    L,F,P=_proceeds(principal,prepaid_fees)
    r=_number(note_rate,"note rate")
    if F==0:
        return _report(r,"periodic cost")
    if periods==1:
        return _report(r+F*(1+r)/P,"periodic cost")

    # Normalize PV by the contractual payment to avoid large cash amounts.
    target=P*_annuity(r,periods)/L
    try:
        with localcontext(Context(prec=750)):
            target=Decimal(target.numerator)/Decimal(target.denominator)
            lo=Decimal(r.numerator)/Decimal(r.denominator)
            hi=1/target
            for _ in range(4096):
                mid=lo+(hi-lo)/2
                if hi-lo<=max(mid*Decimal("1e-20"),Decimal("1e-350")):
                    return _report(mid,"periodic cost")
                value=(1-(1+mid)**-periods)/mid
                if value>target:
                    lo=mid
                else:
                    hi=mid
    except DecimalException as exc:
        raise ValueError("periodic cost outside supported range") from exc
    raise ValueError("periodic cost did not converge")

def annual_rates(periodic_rate,periods_per_year=12):
    _count(periods_per_year)
    r=_number(periodic_rate,"periodic rate")
    nominal=_report(r*periods_per_year,"nominal annual rate")
    if periods_per_year==1 or r==0:
        return nominal,nominal
    try:
        effective=math.expm1(periods_per_year*math.log1p(float(r)))
    except OverflowError as exc:
        raise ValueError("effective annual rate outside supported range") from exc
    return nominal,_report(effective,"effective annual rate")

def borrowing_cost(L,r,n,fee):
    return periodic_irr(L,r,n,fee)

def effective_annual(r,m=12):
    return annual_rates(r,m)[1]

class ExerciseChecks(unittest.TestCase):
    def test_01_zero_rate(self):self.assertEqual(payment(1200,0,12),100)
    def test_02_monthly_rate(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(.06/12,.005)
    def test_03_payment(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(payment(10000,.005,24),10000*.005/(1-1.005**-24))
    def test_04_first_interest(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(schedule(10000,.005,24)[0][2],50)
    def test_05_first_principal(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(schedule(10000,.005,24)[0][4],payment(10000,.005,24)-50)
    def test_06_balance(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(balance(10000,.005,24,1),schedule(10000,.005,24)[0][-1])
    def test_07_maturity(self):self.assertEqual(balance(10000,.005,24,24),0)
    def test_08_principal(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(sum(x[4] for x in schedule(10000,.005,24)),10000)
    def test_09_prepayment(self):
        self.assertLess(sum(x[2] for x in schedule(10000,.005,24,100)),sum(x[2] for x in schedule(10000,.005,24)))
    def test_10_ltv(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(240000/300000,.8)
    def test_11_effective(self):self.assertAlmostEqual(effective_annual(.01),1.01**12-1)
    def test_12_fee(self):
        self.assertGreater(borrowing_cost(10000,.005,24,200),.005)
        self.assertEqual(borrowing_cost(1,0,1,1e-100),1e-100)
        with self.assertRaises(ValueError): borrowing_cost(1000,.01,12,float("nan"))

if __name__=="__main__":
    b=schedule(240000,.06/12,360)
    e=schedule(240000,.06/12,360,200)
    print("Baseline:",len(b),"payments, interest",round(sum(x[2] for x in b),2))
    print("Accelerated:",len(e),"payments, interest",round(sum(x[2] for x in e),2))
    base_interest=math.fsum(row[2] for row in b)
    extra_interest=math.fsum(row[2] for row in e)
    principal=Fraction(200000)
    old=principal/_annuity(Fraction(".005"),240)
    new=principal/_annuity(Fraction(".00375"),240)
    saving=old-new
    print(f"Monthly payment: {b[0][3]:.2f}; first interest: {b[0][2]:.2f}")
    print(f"Interest saved: {base_interest-extra_interest:.2f}; periods saved: {len(b)-len(e)}")
    print(f"Refinance: {float(old):.2f} -> {float(new):.2f}; monthly savings: {float(saving):.2f}; simple break-even: {math.ceil(3000/saving)}")
    print("Reconciled:",reconciles(b,240000),reconciles(e,240000))
    unittest.main(verbosity=2)
```
